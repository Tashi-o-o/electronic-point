Decoding the Sony Red Light Blinking Fault

A classic issue with Sony TVs is the sudden transition to a black screen accompanied by a blinking red standby light. This is Sony's built-in self-diagnosis protection mode. The number of times the light flashes (e.g., 2 blinks, 4 blinks, or 6 blinks) tells a trained technician exactly which component has failed—whether it is the main board, power supply unit, or the backlight inverter. Our team reads these codes instantly to trace and isolate the issue efficiently.
