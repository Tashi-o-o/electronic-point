Is Your TV Screen Worth Replacing?

When a television suffers impact damage or panel failure, many customers wonder if they should just buy a new TV. In most cases, especially for large smart TVs (43-inch, 55-inch, and 65-inch) and premium models from Samsung, Sony, or LG, replacing the panel is a highly cost-effective solution.

Signs You Need a Panel Replacement

  • Physical Cracks: Visible spiderweb cracks indicating physical impact to the display glass.
  • Vertical or Horizontal Lines: Colorful lines running across the screen, often caused by failing COF (Chip on Film) bonding.
  • Double Image or Ghosting: The picture stutters or duplicates vertically, a common panel timing fault.
  • Half Screen Dark: One side of the screen is completely black or distorted.
