Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our repair process, costs, and service turnaround times.

How much does a home TV inspection cost in Mansarovar?
Our doorstep diagnostic inspection is highly affordable. We visit your home in Mansarovar, Narayan Vihar, or surrounding areas to identify the exact issue and provide a transparent repair estimate before any work begins.
Do you fix TVs with sound but no picture?
Yes, this is one of the most common issues we see. A TV with sound but a black screen usually indicates a failed LED backlight or inverter board. We can replace these components securely at your home or in our workshop.
How long does motherboard or backlight repair take?
Most LED backlight replacements and motherboard repairs are completed within the same day. Depending on part availability for specific Sony, Samsung, or LG models, we aim for the fastest turnaround possible.
