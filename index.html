Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our repair process, warranties, and service areas.

Do you provide a warranty on your repairs?
Yes, absolutely! We provide a full 1-Year Warranty for Display Replacements (Note: Warranty is voided in cases of water damage or physical breakage). We also provide a standard, reliable warranty on all other replaced parts so you can have complete peace of mind.
Which areas in Jaipur do you serve?
We proudly serve All of Jaipur! While our workshop is based in Mansarovar, our mobile technicians cover Vaishali Nagar, Malviya Nagar, Sodala, Tonk Road, Pratap Nagar, Jagatpura, and every neighborhood in between.
Do I need to bring my TV to your shop?
No need! We provide convenient doorstep service. Our technicians will visit your home with the necessary tools and parts to diagnose and fix the issue right in your living room.
