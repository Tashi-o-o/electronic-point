WebOS & Magic Remote Troubleshooting

Is your LG TV stuck on the logo screen, dropping Wi-Fi constantly, or failing to pair with your Magic Remote? These are common WebOS and Bluetooth module failures. OLED & NanoCell Hardware Repair

If you are experiencing vertical lines, sound with no picture, or unexpected power-offs, your LG TV likely has a backlight, T-Con board, or panel failure. Waiting for e-commerce return windows or manufacturer support can leave you without a screen for weeks. We offer local, rapid hardware replacements using high-quality components.

Common LG Issues We Resolve:

  • TV turning on and immediately shutting off (Power Supply failure).

  • "No Signal" errors on active HDMI ports.
